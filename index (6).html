We design and deploy robust network infrastructures that form the backbone of modern enterprises. Our expertise spans multi-vendor environments including Cisco, MikroTik, Aruba, Ruijie, and Ubiquiti, allowing us to recommend the optimal solution for your specific requirements and budget.

Our network designs prioritise resilience, scalability, and performance. We implement redundant topologies with HSRP and VRRP for gateway redundancy, combined with link aggregation for high availability. Every design includes proper VLAN segmentation to isolate traffic types and improve security posture.

Our wireless deployments leverage controller-based architectures from Aruba and Cisco to provide seamless roaming, centralised management, and advanced security features. We perform RF site surveys, heat mapping, and ongoing optimisation to ensure consistent coverage.

Network Automation

CI/CD pipelines and Infrastructure-as-Code for network operations

Key Capabilities

✓CI/CD Pipeline Development (GitHub Actions, GitLab)
✓Configuration Management (Ansible)
✓Network Testing & Validation (pyATS, Genie)
✓Infrastructure-as-Code for Networks
✓Custom Automation Scripts (Python)
✓API Integration (REST, NETCONF, gRPC)
✓Automated Compliance Checking
✓Documentation Generation & Version Control

Network automation transforms how infrastructure is deployed, configured, and maintained. We build comprehensive CI/CD pipelines that treat network configurations as code, enabling rapid deployments with reduced human error and complete audit trails.

Our automation frameworks leverage Ansible for configuration management, combined with pyATS and Genie for pre- and post-deployment validation. We integrate with GitHub Actions or GitLab CI/CD, providing a developer-friendly workflow where network engineers can propose changes via pull requests, trigger automated testing, and deploy only after peer review.

Beyond configuration, we automate operational tasks including compliance checking, backup management, and documentation generation. Our Python-based solutions interact with device APIs, parse operational data, and generate reports for real-time visibility into network health.

RSquared Technologies was founded in Sandton, Johannesburg, with a simple mission: to bring enterprise-grade IT infrastructure, security, and automation to organisations across South Africa — delivered with the rigour and precision of a hands-on engineer.

What sets us apart is depth. We don't just specify and hand over — we design, deploy, monitor, and automate. From VLAN segmentation across multi-vendor environments to building full CI/CD pipelines for network infrastructure, we operate at the intersection of traditional networking and modern DevOps.

Our approach combines deep technical certifications (CCNP Security, CCNP Enterprise, AWS SysOps, Fortinet NSE, Cisco DevNet, and more) with real-world operational experience across SOC/NOC monitoring, incident response, firewall management, and cloud automation.

We believe that exceptional IT infrastructure is invisible when it works and invaluable when it matters. Every solution we deliver is architected for resilience, documented for clarity, and automated for consistency.
