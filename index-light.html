Network Consultation

Vendor-neutral assessment and design of your network architecture.

Key Capabilities

✓Architecture & capacity planning
✓LAN, WAN & Wi-Fi design
✓Multi-vendor network audits
✓Scalability & performance benchmarking
✓Structured cabling planning
✓Network segmentation strategy
✓Redundancy & high-availability design
✓Technology roadmap development

Our Network Consultation practice starts with your environment, not a vendor catalogue. We assess your current LAN, WAN, and wireless estate — identifying coverage gaps, capacity constraints, and single points of failure — before recommending a right-sized architecture.

Whether you're planning a new site, refreshing ageing infrastructure, or preparing for growth, we deliver clear, documented designs your own team can implement and maintain, with vendor-neutral recommendations grounded in hands-on engineering experience rather than sales targets.

Security Consultation

Risk assessments and security strategy aligned to best practice and regulation.

Key Capabilities

✓Risk & maturity assessments
✓Security policy & governance development
✓Compliance guidance (ISO 27001, POPIA)
✓Security architecture review
✓Zero-trust strategy design
✓Incident response planning
✓Security awareness programme design
✓Vendor & third-party risk review

We help organisations understand where they actually stand: assessing security maturity against recognised frameworks, identifying gaps in policy and practice, and prioritising remediation by real business risk rather than checklist compliance.

Every engagement produces a practical roadmap — not just a findings report — covering governance, incident response readiness, and regulatory alignment, so your leadership team can make informed decisions about where to invest next.

Network & Security Infrastructure

End-to-end deployment and management of firewalls, switches, routers, and VPNs.

Key Capabilities

✓Firewall deployment & hardening
✓VPN & secure remote access
✓Switching & routing infrastructure
✓Network segmentation & VLANs
✓Wireless infrastructure deployment
✓High-availability & failover configuration
✓Ongoing monitoring & management
✓Change control & documentation

We design and deploy the infrastructure that carries your business — firewalls, switches, routers, and VPNs — engineered to work as one secure system rather than a collection of disconnected boxes.

Beyond initial rollout, we provide ongoing management: policy tuning, firmware and patch management, capacity monitoring, and documented change control, so your network stays secure and predictable as it grows.

Penetration Testing

Real-world attack simulations across your network, applications, and people.

Key Capabilities

✓Internal & external network testing
✓Web application testing
✓Wireless security assessment
✓Social engineering & phishing simulations
✓Vulnerability scanning & prioritisation
✓Configuration & hardening review
✓Detailed findings & remediation reports
✓Retesting & validation

We simulate the tactics real attackers use — from the internet-facing perimeter to the internal network, web applications, wireless estate, and your people — to find weaknesses before someone else does.

Every engagement ends with a clear, prioritised report: what we found, how we got there, and exactly how to fix it — followed by retesting to confirm remediation actually closed the gap.

About Us

Built by engineers.
Trusted by enterprise.

The story of RSquared IT Technologies — from hands-on engineering to end-to-end infrastructure ownership.

Engineering-first,
client-focused.

RSquared IT Technologies was founded in Johannesburg, South Africa, with a simple mission: bring enterprise-grade network infrastructure and cybersecurity to organisations of every size, delivered with the rigour and precision of a hands-on engineering team.

What sets us apart is depth. We don't just specify and hand over — we consult, design, deploy, test, and support. From initial network consultation through to penetration testing and hardware supply, we stay involved end-to-end so nothing gets lost between the strategy deck and the server rack.

Our approach combines deep technical certifications with real-world operational experience across network engineering, security consulting, and infrastructure delivery — so every recommendation is grounded in practice, not vendor literature.
